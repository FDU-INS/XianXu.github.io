<div class="word-intro">
<p>Professor and doctoral supervisor at Fudan University. He currently serves as <strong>Vice Dean of the School of Economics, Fudan University</strong>; Head of the Department of Risk Management and Insurance; Vice Dean of the Fudan Institute of Insurance Application and Innovation; and Deputy Director of the Ministry of Education Laboratory of Philosophy and Social Sciences (Silver-Age Futures). He is the <strong>Chief Expert of a Major Project of the National Social Science Fund of China</strong> and concurrently serves as <strong>Vice President of the Insurance Society of China (a national first-level academic society)</strong>; a member of the Ministry of Education’s <strong>National Steering Committee for Graduate Education in Insurance</strong>; Vice President of the Shanghai Insurance Society; and <strong>Deputy Editor-in-Chief of a core textbook under the Ministry of Education’s “101 Plan”</strong>. He also serves on the editorial boards of leading Chinese and international journals, including <em>Disaster Prevention and Management</em> (SSCI) and <em>Insurance Studies</em> (CSSCI).</p>

<p>His research focuses on three broad areas: health and ageing, human capital and household finance; financial technology, insurance technology and AI for Science; and industrial innovation and global risk governance. He examines how insurance systems contribute to national governance and people’s welfare amid economic and social transformation and technological change. His papers have been published or accepted in leading Chinese and international journals, including <strong><em>Journal of Development Economics, Journal of Risk and Insurance, Journal of Economic Dynamics and Control, Journal of Banking and Finance</em></strong>, <em>Insurance: Mathematics and Economics</em>, <em>China Economic Review</em>, <strong><em>Journal of Financial Research</em></strong>, <strong><em>Accounting Research</em></strong> and <em>The Journal of Quantitative &amp; Technical Economics</em>, as well as at <strong>leading international AI conferences such as ICCV</strong>. He contributed the first full chapter on China to the <strong><em>Handbook of Insurance</em>, an authoritative international reference in insurance</strong>. He has led a major project of the National Social Science Fund of China, a General Program project of the National Natural Science Foundation of China, multiple key provincial and ministerial research projects, and major commissioned research projects.</p>
